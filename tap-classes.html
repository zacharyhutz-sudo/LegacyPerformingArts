Class Details

How tap progresses at Legacy.

Pre-Dance B is the first listed program to include tap, giving ages 4–5 an introduction alongside ballet and creative movement. Combo A and B continue tap together with ballet and jazz.

Pre-Company dancers ages 10 and up have a dedicated Tuesday tap class. Junior and Senior Company dancers take dedicated tap classes on Thursday as part of their company schedule.

Junior and Senior Company members are required to take at least ballet, tap, and jazz. Individual class pricing is available by request for dancers who are not participating in the full company program.

Common Questions

Before your dancer gets started.

What age does tap start?

The current class breakdown first lists tap in Pre-Dance B for ages 4–5.

Is tap included in Combo classes?

Yes. Combo A and Combo B include ballet, tap, and jazz.

When do company dancers take tap?

Pre-Company tap is Tuesday. Junior and Senior Company tap classes are listed on Thursday.

Do I have to join company to take classes?

No. Legacy states that dancers are not required to be part of a company to participate in classes.
