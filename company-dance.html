Class Details

Requirements, optional classes, and recital fees.

Junior and Senior Company members are required to take at least ballet, tap, and jazz. Senior Company also includes pointe in the current weekly schedule.

For company members, the addition of one optional class is listed at $10, and each additional class after that is $5. Optional class pricing for other situations can be provided by request.

The current recital fee per recital is $150 for Pre-Company, $185 for Junior Company, and $200 for Senior Company. It is not required that a dancer be part of a company to participate in classes at Legacy.

Common Questions

Before your dancer gets started.

What age can a dancer join company?

Company classes begin at age 10 with auditions. Pre-Company is listed for ages 10+, Junior Company for ages 12+, and Senior Company for ages 14+.

What classes are required?

Junior and Senior Company members are required to take at least ballet, tap, and jazz.

How much is company tuition?

For 2026–2027, Pre-Company is $80 per month, Junior Company is $155 per month, and Senior Company is $160 per month.

Do dancers have to join company to take classes?

No. Legacy states that dancers do not have to be part of a company to participate in classes.
