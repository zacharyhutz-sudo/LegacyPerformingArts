Class Details

How jazz fits into the Legacy schedule.

Combo A and Combo B introduce structured jazz training alongside ballet and tap for ages 6–9. This gives dancers experience across all three core styles in one program.

Pre-Company includes a dedicated 45-minute jazz class on Tuesday. Junior and Senior Company dancers continue with longer dedicated jazz classes on Thursday.

Junior and Senior Company members are required to take at least ballet, tap, and jazz. Individual class pricing can be provided by request for dancers outside the full company package.
