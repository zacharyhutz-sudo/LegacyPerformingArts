Classes

A place to start, grow, and shine.

Legacy Performing Arts offers training for developing dancers at every stage, from first classes through company-level performance.

Find your fit

Training designed to grow with your dancer.

Classes are offered throughout the week with options for younger dancers, combination classes, company training, and additional styles. If you are unsure where your dancer belongs, contact the studio and we can help point you in the right direction.

Core Classes

Build a strong foundation.

01

Pre-Dance A

An encouraging introduction to movement, rhythm, classroom confidence, and foundational dance skills for younger dancers.

02

Pre-Dance B

Age-appropriate foundational training that builds coordination, musicality, listening skills, and comfort in the studio.

03

Combo A

A multi-style class that helps developing dancers strengthen technique while gaining experience across complementary dance disciplines.

04

Combo B

Continued combination training focused on stronger technique, musicality, confidence, and performance readiness.

Company Training

For dancers ready for more.

01

Pre-Company

A bridge into company training for dancers ready for increased focus, technique development, and performance expectations.

02

Junior Company

Focused company training that develops technique, discipline, artistry, teamwork, and confidence on stage.

03

Senior Company

Advanced training for committed dancers pursuing stronger technique, versatility, artistry, and performance experience.

Additional Training

Add another style to the week.

Contemporary

Available as an additional class for company and non-company dancers, with expressive movement and contemporary technique.

Hip Hop

An energetic add-on class focused on rhythm, style, musicality, confidence, and dynamic movement.

Private Lessons

One-on-one instruction is available by appointment for dancers who want focused help with technique, skills, or individual goals.
