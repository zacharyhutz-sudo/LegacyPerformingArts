2026–2027 Details

Company requirements and recital fees.

Junior and Senior Company members are required to take at least ballet, tap, and jazz. It is not required that a dancer be part of a company to participate in classes.

Recital fee per recital: Pre-Dance $60, Combo $70, Pre-Company $150, Junior Company $185, Senior Company $200. Individual class and optional class pricing is available by request.
