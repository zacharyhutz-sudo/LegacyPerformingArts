Current 2026–2027 Program

Two preschool programs, designed by age.

For the 2026–2027 season, Legacy offers Pre-Dance A for ages 2–3 and Pre-Dance B for ages 4–5. Both programs are designed to introduce young dancers to the studio in a manageable class length while building comfort with music and movement.

Class Details

What families can expect from Pre-Dance.

Pre-Dance A introduces younger dancers to ballet and creative movement in a 30-minute class. The shorter format gives ages 2–3 a focused first experience in the studio.

Pre-Dance B expands the experience to ballet, tap, and creative movement for ages 4–5. Families can choose a Tuesday or Wednesday option based on the current schedule.

The current recital fee for Pre-Dance is $60 per recital. Class offerings are subject to change depending on interest, so families should check the studio schedule or contact Legacy before registering.
